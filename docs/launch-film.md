# Flare launch film

The 28-second English launch film demonstrates the current application using an original procedural example and real editor captures. It is a product overview, not a real-time recording of an AI generation session.

## Production

1. Create an English two-shot example with Flare's original Paper narrative renderer.
2. Capture the actual editor and a frame from the shared preview/export renderer. Use isolated demo data, not a user's private projects.
3. Prepare five original title cards using those captures.
4. Import the cards into a 28-second Flare project, with shot durations of 5, 6, 6, 6 and 5 seconds.
5. Export the project through Flare's image-assisted renderer and normal 1080p export queue.
6. Finish the brand strip and original synthesized music in FFmpeg. This finishing step is external to Flare; the release does not claim built-in title-card design or custom music composition.

No paid AI generation was used for this launch film. The artwork, title cards and score were prepared specifically for Flare. No Henge footage, upstream demo film or third-party music is included. The Henge launch film informed the demonstration structure only.

The public MP4 is H.264/AAC, 1920 × 1080, 24 fps. Interface captures show actual controls; captions describe supported capabilities. English/Chinese interface switching does not translate user-authored scripts.
