# Flare launch post

I built a video tool. Then used it to make its own launch film.

Meet Flare—an open-source workspace that takes a script through storyboarding, shot-by-shot editing, preview and export, all on your machine.

The part I care about: control after the first draft.

Rewrite one shot. Change its timing. Swap an image. Reorder the story. Preview it again. You shouldn't have to restart an entire film because one moment doesn't work.

Bring your own DeepSeek key for planning and rewrites, and DreamAPI for images and narration. Or start without a key using local storyboards and procedural animation.

99 visual-style references. English and Chinese. Light and dark. Landscape, portrait and square. MP4, subtitles and editable project exports.

This is an early release. The 99 styles are references with different levels of rendering support—not 99 one-click movie engines.

The video below went through Flare's export pipeline, with locally prepared title cards and final sound design.

Code is open. Try it, break it, tell me which part of making a short film still feels harder than it should.

https://github.com/Hy-1990/Flare
